**Subject:** Research access request: 2023 Coimbatore smart solar microgrid dataset

Dear Dr Sivaramkrishnan,

I am developing ReCell, a research project investigating how used EV battery
health forecasts can inform solar-storage operation. I read your paper,
"Optimization based load forecasting and demand management in smart building
microgrids with Greylag Goose and Bi level graph models"
(DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-36960-x). Its data-availability statement invites requests
to the corresponding author.

Could you share a public repository link or provide research access to the 2023
five-minute records from the Hindusthan College of Engineering and Technology
smart solar microgrid described in the paper?

The most useful inputs for our independent validation would be:

- Timestamped building/grid demand, measured PV generation, battery charging and
  discharging measurements, and any recorded battery SOC and temperature channels.
- A data dictionary covering units, native timezone, interval meaning, import/
  export and charging/discharging signs, meter topology, and equipment ratings.
- Raw readings and original missing/quality flags, if available, together with
  descriptions of filtering, interpolation or normalization already applied.
- Battery chemistry, nominal/usable capacity and inverter limits, if shareable;
  optional tariff category, contract demand or redacted billing totals would help
  distinguish technical replay from bill validation.

If some channels were not collected or cannot be shared, an explicit list of
available fields would still be valuable. We do not need personal or occupancy
identifiers.

Please also clarify the dataset's reuse terms for offline research and reporting
aggregate validation results in a client-facing pilot review. We would not assume
that the article license grants permission to redistribute the raw records.

Thank you for considering this request.

Best regards

ReCell project
